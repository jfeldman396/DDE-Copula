iVAE research source
The code under Algorithms/iVAE/vendor is the local research copy of the iVAE
implementation by Ilyes Khemakhem, with its existing MIT License and copyright
notice preserved in Algorithms/iVAE/vendor/LICENSE. Local architecture options
and runtime adaptations are included as used by this study. A verified upstream
commit identifier was not available; MANIFEST.json records the packaged source hashes.

Runtime dependencies
NumPy, SciPy, pandas, h5py, Matplotlib, Pillow and PyTorch are dependencies,
not vendored distributions. Their own licenses apply. Tested versions are
recorded in Scripts/Publication/requirements-*.txt.
MATLAB and its toolboxes are proprietary runtime dependencies and are not bundled.
